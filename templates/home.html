Kasia Suliga

Technology should enhance what makes us human - not replace it

I believe the future of education is not about choosing between humans and technology. It is about creating the right relationship between the two. Technology should remove barriers, extend possibilities and enhance learning - while human connection, communication, creativity, relationships and belonging remain at the heart of education.

I am particularly interested in digital strategy, AI in education, robotics, computational thinking, digital literacy, inclusion and human-centred technology.

My ambition is to help schools move from simply using technology to leading its purposeful, responsible and human-centred adoption.
